## Passage

> **22** After these things, Jesus came with his disciples into the land of Judea. He stayed there with them, and baptized. **23** John also was baptizing in Enon near Salim, because there was much water there. They came, and were baptized. **24** For John was not yet thrown into prison. **25** There arose therefore a questioning on the part of John’s disciples with some Jews about purification. **26** They came to John, and said to him, “Rabbi, he who was with you beyond the Jordan, to whom you have testified, behold, the same baptizes, and everyone is coming to him.” **27** John answered, “A man can receive nothing, unless it has been given him from heaven. **28** You yourselves testify that I said, ‘I am not the Christ,’ but, ‘I have been sent before him.’ **29** He who has the bride is the bridegroom; but the friend of the bridegroom, who stands and hears him, rejoices greatly because of the bridegroom’s voice. This, my joy, therefore is made full. **30** He must increase, but I must decrease. **31** He who comes from above is above all. He who is from the earth belongs to the earth, and speaks of the earth. He who comes from heaven is above all. **32** What he has seen and heard, of that he testifies; and no one receives his witness. **33** He who has received his witness has set his seal to this, that God is true. **34** For he whom God has sent speaks the words of God; for God gives the Spirit without measure. **35** The Father loves the Son, and has given all things into his hand. **36** One who believes in the Son has eternal life, but one who disobeys the Son won’t see life, but the wrath of God remains on him.”

## The Story

John the Baptist’s last public words in John’s Gospel are not a death speech. They are a handoff.

Jesus is baptizing in the Judean countryside. John is still free, still working the water at Aenon near Salim. His own disciples are losing people and they hate it. They come with a complaint dressed as loyalty: the man you pointed to is baptizing, and everyone is going to him.

John will not take the bait. A man can receive only what heaven gives. You already heard me say I am not the Christ. I was sent ahead of him. Then the picture: the bride belongs to the bridegroom. The friend of the bridegroom stands, hears the voice, and is glad. “This, my joy, therefore is made full.” Then the line people search: “He must increase, but I must decrease.”

The rest of the paragraph is why. The one from above is above all. He speaks what he has seen. God gives him the Spirit without measure. The Father has put everything in his hand. Belief in the Son is eternal life.

John 3:30 is the last words of his public ministry in this Gospel. They are not his last words on earth. Herod later locks him up. From prison he sends disciples to ask Jesus if he is the one (Matthew 11:2-6). Then a birthday oath, a grudge, and a platter (Matthew 14:1-12). That death is a different page. This page is the sentence he chose while he could still draw a crowd.

## What It Means

**Decrease is not self-hate. It is job done.** John was never the groom. Joy is hearing the other man’s voice and getting out of the way.

**The complaint is the temptation.** His disciples want him to compete. He refuses a market share reading of the kingdom.

**“Last words” here means last public sermon, not last breath.** Searchers mix this line with the beheading. Keep them straight: John 3:30 is the theology. Matthew 14 is the execution.

**Jesus knew.** When John’s disciples report the prison question, Jesus answers with the works of Isaiah and then tells the crowd no one born of women is greater than John (Matthew 11). He knew John was in the dark. He did not treat him as disposable.

## Questions

### What were John the Baptist's last words?

In John’s Gospel his last public words are “He must increase, but I must decrease” (John 3:30), spoken while he was still baptizing and his crowd was shrinking. They are not a speech from the prison or the block. Matthew 14 records how he died, not a final quote.

### What was John the Baptist's famous quote?

The line people remember is John 3:30: “He must increase, but I must decrease.” He also said he was not the Christ, only the voice ahead of him (John 1:20-23; 3:28), and “Behold, the Lamb of God” (John 1:29).

### What happened to John the Baptist after baptizing Jesus?

He kept baptizing for a time (John 3:23-24). He pointed his own disciples to Jesus, then was arrested by Herod Antipas for condemning Herod’s marriage to Herodias. From prison he sent to ask if Jesus was the one. Herod had him beheaded (Matthew 14:1-12).

### Did Jesus know that John the Baptist died?

Yes. Matthew 14:12-13 says John’s disciples buried him and went and told Jesus. Jesus withdrew by boat to a lonely place. He had already called John more than a prophet (Matthew 11:7-11).
