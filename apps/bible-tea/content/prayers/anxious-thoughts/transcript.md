# When Anxious Thoughts Won't Stop

Lord, my heart feels like a storm, and my mind is a whirlwind of worry. I come to You because I don't know how to calm this chaos on my own. You know every thought before it even forms in my mind, and I feel so overwhelmed by their constant churning. Your word says, "Cast all your anxiety on Him because He cares for you." I long to do this, but the weight is so heavy, and my grip so tight. Help me, God, to release my fears into Your capable hands.

I need Your peace, the peace that surpasses all understanding. The kind of peace that guards my heart and mind, like a fortress in the midst of this storm. I confess that I often rely on my own strength, and it only leaves me wearier. Teach me to trust You more deeply, to lean into Your promises when I feel uncertain and afraid.

Father, remind me that I am not alone. You are with me, even in the darkest moments of my anxiety. Your presence is my refuge. Help me to rest in Your love, knowing that You are my rock and my salvation. Quiet my anxious thoughts, Lord, and fill me with Your perfect peace. Amen.
