# Prayer for a New Beginning

God, I stand before You at a crossroads, feeling the weight of what lies behind and the uncertainty of what awaits. As I prepare to close this chapter and step into the unknown, I ask for Your guidance and wisdom to light my path. Your word reminds me that "in all your ways submit to Him, and He will make your paths straight." I long to trust in that promise, to lean not on my own understanding, but to rest in the assurance that You are with me.

Lord, grant me the courage to embrace this new beginning, to move forward with faith, even when the way isn't clear. Help me to release the fears that hold me back and to trust that You have prepared something beautiful ahead. Let me walk in Your strength, knowing that whatever comes, You have equipped me for this journey.

I bring to You my hopes and dreams, trusting that You will shape them according to Your will. Fill my heart with peace and my spirit with determination. I am grateful for Your constant presence, for being my guide and my refuge. As I step forward, may I always seek Your direction and find comfort in Your unfailing love. Amen.
