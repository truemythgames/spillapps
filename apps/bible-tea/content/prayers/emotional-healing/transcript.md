# Prayer for Emotional Healing

Lord, I come before You with a heart that feels heavy and wounded. You see the scars that lie deep within, those hidden places that no one else can touch. I am reminded of Your promise in Psalm 34:18 that You are close to the brokenhearted and save those who are crushed in spirit. God, I need Your closeness now more than ever. Please touch these invisible wounds with Your healing hands. Help me to release the pain and allow Your peace to fill the spaces where hurt has settled.

You know every tear I've cried and every sleepless night. I ask for the strength to forgive where I need to forgive, to let go of bitterness and anger that entangle my soul. You are the Great Healer, and I trust in Your power to restore my spirit. Wrap me in Your love, Lord, and remind me that I am not alone in this journey. Guide me to Your truth and help me find joy in the midst of sorrow. 

Thank You for being my refuge and my comfort. I place my heart in Your hands, trusting that You will mend it in Your perfect time. Amen.
