# When Healing Doesn't Come

Oh Lord, I come to You weary and burdened, struggling under the weight of unanswered prayers. I have asked for healing, yet my body remains frail and my spirit feels crushed. In my heartache, I remember how You said, "My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness." But right now, Lord, my weakness feels overwhelming, and I long to see Your power at work in my life.

God, I confess my frustration and my doubt. It feels as though You are silent, and I am left in this valley without a clear path forward. Help me to trust that You are here with me, even when I cannot see the evidence of Your presence. Teach me to lean on Your promises that You will never leave me nor forsake me, even in the darkest moments.

Strengthen my spirit, Father, to endure with patience and hope. Let me find comfort in Your love, a love that never fails, and help me to hold on to the belief that You are working all things for my good, even though I cannot understand Your ways. Renew my faith, Lord, and grant me peace that surpasses all understanding. Amen.
