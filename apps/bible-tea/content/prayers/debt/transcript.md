# Freedom from Debt

Dear God, I come to You with a heavy heart, burdened by the weight of my financial struggles. It feels like I'm drowning in debt, and I don't know how to find my way out. I need Your wisdom, Lord, to make the right decisions with what little I have. You say in Proverbs that "the borrower is slave to the lender," and I feel enslaved right now, unable to find peace. Please guide me to financial freedom.

I ask for discipline, Father, to manage my resources wisely. Help me to resist the temptation to spend on what I don't need, and instead, to be a good steward of the blessings You've given me. Teach me patience and perseverance, even when the road ahead seems long and uncertain.

Lord, I pray for breakthroughs in my financial situation. Open doors of opportunity that I cannot see and provide for my needs in unexpected ways. Remind me, God, that Your grace is sufficient, and that I am not alone in this struggle. Give me the courage to trust in Your provision and the faith to believe that You will lead me to a place of stability and peace. 

Amen.
