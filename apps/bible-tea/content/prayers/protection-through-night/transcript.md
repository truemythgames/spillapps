# Protection Through the Night

Heavenly Father, as the day fades away and night envelops us, I come to You seeking Your protection. In the stillness of these hours, I ask that You cover me with Your wings, just as Your Word assures us that "He will cover you with his feathers, and under his wings you will find refuge" (Psalm 91:4). You are my refuge and my fortress, and I place my trust in You alone.

Lord, I pray for Your angels to stand guard around my home tonight. Let Your presence be a shield against any harm that may seek to disturb my rest. In the quiet of the night, when fears and worries often surface, remind me that You are with me, and You never slumber nor sleep.

Grant me peace of mind, dear God, and quiet the racing thoughts that tend to invade at this hour. Help me to release the burdens of this day into Your hands, trusting that You hold tomorrow securely. May Your peace, which surpasses all understanding, guard my heart and mind as I sleep.

Thank You, Lord, for Your constant vigilance and love. I trust in Your protection through this night and every night. Amen.
