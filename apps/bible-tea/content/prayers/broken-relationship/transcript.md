# A Broken Relationship

Father, I come before You with a heavy heart, knowing the damage that has been done. The bridge between us stands in ruins, and I feel the weight of brokenness pressing down on my soul. I confess my part in this fracture, for my words and actions have not always reflected Your love. I long for restoration, but it feels so far beyond my reach. God, I remember that You are the God who reconciles, the one who mends what seems impossible to repair. Colossians tells me to forgive as You have forgiven me, yet my heart struggles to release the hurt and anger. Help me to embody Your grace, even when it feels like I am giving away a piece of myself. Lord, soften our hearts, mine and theirs, to allow Your peace to seep into the cracks of our pain. Show me the steps I need to take, the words I need to speak, and the silence I need to hold. I trust in Your power to heal and restore. Teach me to be patient as I wait for Your perfect timing. Amen.
