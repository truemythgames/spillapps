# Walking in Purpose

Heavenly Father, as the dawn breaks and a new day begins, I come before You seeking Your guidance. I know that each day is a gift from You, filled with opportunities to serve and glorify Your name. Help me to walk intentionally in the purpose You have set before me. Your Word tells me that "many are the plans in a person’s heart, but it is the Lord’s purpose that prevails." Teach me to align my heart with Your will, that I might not simply be busy, but intentional in all I do.

Grant me wisdom to discern the tasks that truly matter and the courage to let go of distractions. Let Your Spirit lead me to those who need a touch of Your love and kindness today. Open my eyes to see the needs around me and give me the strength to meet them with grace and humility.

Father, I trust that You have prepared good works for me to walk in today. May my actions reflect Your love and my words bring life and encouragement to those I encounter. As I step forward, let my life be a testament to Your goodness and mercy. Keep my heart attuned to Your voice and my feet steady on the path You have laid out for me. Amen.
