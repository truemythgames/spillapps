# Prayer for Your Children

Heavenly Father, I come before You with a heart full of love and concern for my children. You know the depths of my hopes and fears for them. I ask You to be their shield and protector. In a world filled with uncertainty, guide their steps and guard their hearts. I acknowledge that I can't shelter them from every storm, but I entrust them into Your mighty hands, believing that You are their refuge.

Lord, Your Word says, "Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old, he will not depart from it." Help me to guide them with wisdom and love, planting seeds of faith that will grow strong in their hearts. Give me the patience and understanding to nurture their unique gifts and to be a living example of Your grace.

Father, when I feel overwhelmed by the responsibility of parenting, remind me that You are with me. Grant me peace in moments of worry and strength when I feel weary. Help me to release my anxieties to You, trusting that You love my children even more than I do.

Surround them with Your angels, Lord, and fill their lives with Your presence. May they grow to know You deeply and trust in Your unfailing love. Amen.
