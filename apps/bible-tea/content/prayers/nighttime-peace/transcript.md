# Nighttime Peace

Dear God, as the day draws to a close, I come to You with a heart full of gratitude and burdens that I long to lay down. You know every detail of my day, the moments of joy and the struggles that linger in my mind. I ask for Your peace to wash over me, calming my restless thoughts and bringing stillness to my soul. Your word reminds me, "He gives to His beloved sleep." May I trust in that promise tonight. 

Help me release the weight of worry and anxiety, allowing Your gentle presence to cradle me into deep rest. I long for my mind to be quieted, free from the noise of the world, so that I may hear Your whispers of love and assurance. Guide my dreams, Lord, and fill them with Your light and hope. 

May my sleep be a time of renewal, a sacred space where You restore my strength and prepare me for the day ahead. I surrender my fears and uncertainties to You, confident in Your protection throughout the night. In Your arms, I find safety and comfort, knowing that You watch over me with tender care. Amen.
