# When You Feel Alone

Oh Lord, I come to You with a heavy heart, feeling the weight of loneliness pressing down on me. In this quiet space where my phone doesn’t ring and my door remains closed, I am reminded of how much I need Your presence. Father, You know my thoughts and my fears, and You see the tears that fall when no one is watching. You promise in Psalm 34:18 that You are close to the brokenhearted and save those who are crushed in spirit. I hold on to this truth, knowing that even in my solitude, I am never truly alone with You by my side.

God, help me to see the connections I do have, the friendships that may be distant but are still real. Open my eyes to new opportunities for community and fellowship. Grant me the courage to reach out when I feel isolated, and the strength to accept the love others offer. Teach me patience and trust in Your perfect timing, knowing that Your plans for me are for good, to give me hope and a future.

Let Your love fill the empty spaces within me, Lord. Remind me that I am seen, known, and cherished by You. Thank You for being my constant companion, my refuge, and my strength. Amen.
