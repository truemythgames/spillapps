# Prayer for Endurance

Oh Lord, my strength feels depleted, and the path ahead seems unending. In this moment of weariness, I turn to You, my refuge and my fortress. You have promised that those who hope in You will renew their strength, and I cling to this promise. Help me to run and not be weary, to walk and not faint. My heart is heavy with doubt and my legs with fatigue, yet I know that through You, I can do all things. Father, when the journey seems impossible, remind me that You are the source of my endurance and courage. Your Word tells me that Your power is made perfect in weakness, and so I lay my frailties before You, trusting in Your mighty strength to carry me through. Lift my spirit with the assurance that You are with me, every step of the way. Let Your steadfast love be the wind beneath my wings, propelling me forward when I feel like giving up. Thank You for being my rock, my ever-present help in times of trouble. Amen.
