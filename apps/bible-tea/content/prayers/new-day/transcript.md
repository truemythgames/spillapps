# A New Day

Lord, as the dawn breaks and a new day begins, I come before You with a heart full of gratitude. Thank You for the breath in my lungs and the promise of fresh mercies. Your faithfulness is new every morning, and I am reminded of Your unfailing love. Today, I ask for Your guidance in each step I take. Let my words reflect Your truth and my actions be filled with Your grace. 

Father, You know the challenges I may face and the decisions I must make. Grant me wisdom and discernment, that I might walk in a way that honors You. As I carry the burdens of yesterday, I lay them at Your feet, trusting that You make all things new. Your word says that Your mercies are new every morning, and in this promise, I find hope and strength.

Help me to see others through Your eyes, to be a vessel of Your love and peace. May I be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to anger, shining Your light in every corner of my life. Thank You for this new day and the opportunity to start afresh. Guide me, Lord, and let Your presence be my comfort. Amen.
