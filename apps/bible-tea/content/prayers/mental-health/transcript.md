# A Prayer for Your Mental Health

God, I come to You because my mind feels like a storm I can't calm. The darkness wraps around me, and it's hard to see a way out. You know my thoughts, even when they race and spiral out of control. You say in Your Word that You are near to the brokenhearted and save those who are crushed in spirit. I cling to that promise, Lord, because I feel crushed under the weight of my own mind.

I ask for Your peace, the peace that surpasses all understanding, to guard my heart and mind. I need Your light to pierce through this fog. Help me to remember that I am not alone, that even in the depths, Your hand is there to guide me. Reassure me of Your presence when everything else feels uncertain.

Father, grant me the strength to reach out for help, to speak about the unspeakable things that haunt me. Give me the courage to face each day, even when I feel numb and disconnected. You are my refuge and strength, an ever-present help in trouble. Let me find rest in You, Lord, when my soul is weary. Heal my mind, restore my spirit, and renew my hope. Amen.
