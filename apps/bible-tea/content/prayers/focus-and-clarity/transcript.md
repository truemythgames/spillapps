# Focus & Clarity

Father, as the dawn breaks and a new day unfolds, I come to You seeking focus and clarity. My mind is often cluttered with thoughts and distractions, yet I long for a stillness that only You can provide. Your word reminds me in James 1:5 that if I lack wisdom, I should ask of You who gives generously to all without finding fault. So, Lord, I ask for Your wisdom today. 

Guide my thoughts and align my actions with Your will. Help me discern what is essential and what can be set aside. Keep my heart steady when the world around me is chaotic. Teach me to find moments of quiet in the busyness, and let those moments be filled with Your presence. 

Lord, I trust that You have already gone before me, preparing the way. Help me to walk in confidence, knowing that I am not alone. Give me eyes to see the opportunities You place before me and the strength to pursue them with integrity and purpose.

Thank You for the gift of this new day. May it be one where I reflect Your love and grace in all I do. Amen.
