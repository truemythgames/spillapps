# Surrendering This Day

Lord, as the sun rises, I come to You with a heart eager yet sometimes weary. I lay this day before You, acknowledging that without Your guidance, my steps falter. Your word reminds me that "Your mercies are new every morning" and I am grateful for the fresh start You offer. Today, I surrender my plans, my worries, and my ambitions to You. I often get caught up in the busyness, forgetting to seek Your will first. Help me to prioritize You above all else, trusting that You will direct my path.

Give me the wisdom to discern what truly matters and the courage to let go of what does not. Equip me with patience and kindness in my interactions, so that I may reflect Your love to those I meet. When challenges arise, remind me of Your strength within me, for apart from You, I can do nothing. Let my actions today be a testament to Your goodness, and may my words bring peace and encouragement.

Guide me, Lord, through each moment, and fill my heart with a peace that surpasses understanding. I place this day entirely in Your hands. Amen.
