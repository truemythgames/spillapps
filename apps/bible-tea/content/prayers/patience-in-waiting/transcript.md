# Patience in Waiting

God, I come to You with a heart full of questions and a mind that is restless. I am in this place of waiting, and it feels so unbearable at times. I know that Your ways are higher than mine, and Your timing is perfect, yet I struggle to find peace in this delay. I feel the weight of uncertainty pressing upon me, and it is hard to hold on to patience when the answers seem so far away.

You remind me through Your word that "those who wait on the Lord shall renew their strength." I need that strength now more than ever, Lord. I need the courage to trust in Your promises and the grace to accept that Your plans for me are good, even when I cannot see the path ahead.

Please help me to turn my anxious thoughts into prayers and my doubts into trust. I long to lean on Your everlasting arms, to find rest in Your presence as I wait. Grant me the patience to endure this season, knowing that You are working all things together for my good. Amen.
