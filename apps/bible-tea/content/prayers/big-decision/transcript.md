# Facing a Big Decision

Lord, You know the weight that sits on my heart as I stand at this crossroads. I feel torn and uncertain, not knowing which path to choose. Your Word tells me that if I lack wisdom, I should ask You, who gives generously to all without finding fault. Here I am, asking, seeking Your guidance and clarity. You know the desires and fears that swirl within me. You see the future that I cannot. I long to trust in Your understanding rather than leaning on my limited perspective. Help me to acknowledge You in all my ways, trusting that You will make my paths straight. Grant me peace in the waiting, and courage to follow the direction You reveal. Steady my heart, Lord, when anxiety whispers doubt. Fill me with Your presence and remind me that wherever I go, You are with me. I place my decision in Your hands, believing that You are working all things together for good. Lead me, Father, with Your gentle and unwavering hand. Amen.
