# Morning Gratitude

Heavenly Father, as the morning light breaks through the darkness, I lift my heart to You in gratitude. Thank You for the gift of a new day, a fresh start filled with possibilities. Your mercies are new every morning, and I am so grateful for the breath in my lungs and the beat of my heart. Your faithfulness, Lord, reaches to the skies, and I am reminded that Your love never fails.

Thank You for the beauty of creation that surrounds me, for the sun that rises faithfully each day, reminding me of Your constant presence. I praise You for the small wonders and the grand miracles that fill my life. Help me to see them clearly today, to walk with eyes wide open to Your goodness and grace.

Lord, as the psalmist said, "This is the day that the Lord has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it." I choose to enter this day with joy and anticipation, knowing that You hold each moment in Your hands. Let my heart overflow with gratitude for the blessings seen and unseen, trusting that You are working all things for my good.

Guide my steps, Lord, and fill my heart with Your peace and joy. May my life reflect Your light as I move through the day in thankfulness. Amen.
