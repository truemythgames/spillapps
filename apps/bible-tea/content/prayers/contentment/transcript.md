# Prayer for Contentment

Heavenly Father, I come to You with a heart burdened by financial stress. The weight of my worries has become too much to bear. I feel trapped in a cycle of wanting and needing more, yet never finding peace. Lord, You know my needs before I even speak of them. You have always provided for me, even when I failed to notice Your hand at work. Help me to trust in Your provision and to remember that "godliness with contentment is great gain" (1 Timothy 6:6).

Father, guide my heart away from the endless pursuit of more, and teach me to rest in the present moment. Open my eyes to the blessings that already surround me, and help me to find joy in the simple and the small. I long to release my grip on the things of this world and to hold tightly to You instead. Please grant me the wisdom to manage what I have with gratitude and faithfulness.

Lord, remind me that true wealth is found not in abundance but in Your presence. May I seek Your kingdom first, trusting that everything else will be given according to Your perfect will. Thank You for being my refuge and my strength, a constant help in times of trouble. Amen.
