# Standing Alone

God, I feel like I'm standing on a lonely island, surrounded by a sea of voices that tell me to bend and break. I am the last one standing firm, and the weight of this solitude presses heavily on my heart. You promise that You will uphold me with Your righteous right hand, just as You said in Isaiah 41:10. I cling to that promise now, believing that Your strength is made perfect in my weakness. I feel the fear creeping in, the doubts whispering that I should give in, but I look to You for courage, for the power to remain steadfast. You know the struggles I face, the moments where the pressure feels unbearable, yet I trust that You are with me, even in this. Wrap me in Your peace that surpasses all understanding, for I cannot stand alone without You. Fill me with the boldness to speak Your truth in love and to remain unshaken by the waves of compromise. Let Your spirit empower me to be a light in this darkness. Amen.
