# Hope After Loss

Oh Lord, my heart is heavy with sorrow, and the weight of my loss feels unbearable. In the quiet moments, when tears fall and memories flood my mind, I turn to You, seeking comfort only You can provide. Your Word says, "Weeping may endure for a night, but joy comes in the morning." Yet, right now, morning feels so distant, and the night seems endless.

I struggle to believe that joy will find its way back into my life. Help me, God, to hold onto the hope that You've promised. I am like a ship lost at sea, tossed by waves of grief. Be my anchor, steady and sure, when everything else feels uncertain.

You know the depths of my heart, the unspoken pain, the longing for what once was. In my darkest moments, remind me that You are near, collecting my tears, understanding my cries. Give me the strength to face each day, to trust that Your plan holds purpose even when I cannot see it.

Guide me towards healing, Lord. Let Your love be the light that breaks through the shadows of my grief. Help me to believe that joy will return, that Your promises are true, and that morning will come. Amen.
