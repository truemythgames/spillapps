# God's Presence Today

Heavenly Father, as I rise to meet the light of a new day, I ask for Your presence to envelop me. You are my rock and my refuge, and I need Your strength to carry me through whatever today may hold. I am reminded of Your promise that where two or three are gathered in Your name, You are there with them. Though it is just You and me right now, I feel the power of Your presence.

Lord, guide my steps and my thoughts today. Help me to walk in love and to see others as You see them. When challenges arise, be my peace and my wisdom. Let my actions reflect Your grace and my words speak of Your kindness. Fill me with the courage to share Your light in all that I do.

I lay my plans at Your feet, trusting that You will direct my path. In moments of doubt or fear, remind me that You are with me, as You promised in Isaiah, saying, "Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God." Let this assurance be my foundation throughout the day.

Thank You for the gift of today, for the breath in my lungs, and the opportunities to grow closer to You. May Your presence be my constant companion. Amen.
