# Knowing God's Will

Dear God, I come before You feeling overwhelmed by the choices that lie ahead. My heart is restless and my mind is clouded with doubt. I long to hear Your voice guiding me through this labyrinth of decisions. You promise in Your Word that if I lack wisdom, I should ask You, and You will give generously without finding fault. So here I am, Lord, asking for that wisdom. 

Grant me clarity, Father, to discern Your will for my life. Help me to quiet the noise and distractions that pull me away from Your still, small voice. I want to follow the path You have set for me, but I struggle to see the way. Like a lamp to my feet and a light to my path, guide me with Your truth and open my heart to Your leading. 

Teach me to trust in You with all my heart and to not lean on my own understanding. Help me to acknowledge You in all my ways, knowing that You will make my paths straight. Guard me from fear and doubt, and fill me with a peace that surpasses all understanding. I place my trust in You, knowing that You hold my future in Your hands. Amen.
