# Reflect & Release

God, as the day draws to a close, I come before You with a heart full of gratitude and reflection. Thank You for the moments of joy and peace that filled this day. Your faithfulness has been my anchor, and Your mercies have been new every morning, just as You promised in Lamentations 3:22-23. I am grateful for the strength You gave me to face the challenges and for the grace that sustained me through them.

Father, there were moments today when I faltered, when my words or actions did not reflect Your love. I lay these at Your feet, seeking Your forgiveness and guidance. Help me to learn from these moments and to trust in Your ability to transform my weaknesses into strengths.

As I prepare to rest, I release the burdens and worries that cling to my heart. I entrust them to You, knowing that You care for me deeply. Grant me the peace that surpasses all understanding, and let my thoughts dwell on what is true and noble.

Thank You for being my refuge and strength. May Your presence envelop me through the night, and may tomorrow be a new start filled with opportunities to walk closer with You. Amen.
