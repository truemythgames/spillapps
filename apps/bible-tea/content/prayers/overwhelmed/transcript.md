# When You're Completely Overwhelmed

God, I come to You with a heart heavy and burdened. Everything feels like it's closing in, and I can't seem to find solid ground. You know the chaos that surrounds me and the worries that consume my thoughts. It feels like I'm drowning, Lord, and I need Your steady hand to pull me back to the surface. You promise that You are near to the brokenhearted and save those who are crushed in spirit, and today I cling to that promise.

Help me, Father, to release these fears into Your capable hands. I feel trapped in this cycle of anxiety, and I need Your peace, the peace that surpasses all understanding, to guard my heart and mind. Teach me to trust in You with all my heart and not lean on my own understanding, for my understanding is clouded and limited. 

You see the path ahead, even when I cannot. You are my refuge and strength, an ever-present help in trouble. Remind me that I am not alone, that You walk with me through this storm. Fill me with Your courage and surround me with Your love. As I face each moment, help me to breathe and to remember that Your grace is sufficient for me. 

Amen.
