# Gratitude Tonight

Dear God, as I lay down to rest, I come to You with a heart full of gratitude. Thank You for the gift of this day, for the moments of joy and the lessons learned through challenges. I am grateful for the warmth and shelter of my home, a place of peace where I can retreat and find comfort. Thank You for the loved ones You have placed in my life, whose presence reminds me of Your love and care. 

Today, I experienced kindness from a stranger, a reminder that Your goodness flows through unexpected channels. Thank You for the strength in my body that allowed me to fulfill my duties and for the clarity of mind that guided my decisions. I am grateful for the beauty of creation that surrounded me, a testament to Your wondrous works.

As I reflect on these gifts, I remember the words of the Psalmist: "The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want." Your provision is abundant and Your grace is sufficient. Even in moments of worry, You provide. As I close my eyes tonight, fill my dreams with peace and my heart with assurance that You hold tomorrow. Amen.
