# Comfort in Pain

God, my heart is heavy and filled with sorrow. I come to You in my time of grief, seeking Your presence and comfort. You know the depths of my pain and the tears I have shed. I feel lost and overwhelmed, and I need You now more than ever. Your word reminds me that You are close to the brokenhearted and save those who are crushed in spirit. I hold onto that promise, trusting that You are near, even when I struggle to feel Your presence.

Lord, I miss the one I have lost, and the ache seems unbearable at times. Please wrap me in Your love and give me strength to face each day. Help me to find moments of peace amidst the turmoil, and guide me as I navigate this new reality without them by my side. I know that You grieve with me, and I am grateful for Your compassion.

I ask, God, that You would bring healing to my heart. Let Your light shine through the darkness, and help me to lean on You when my own strength fails. Thank You for walking this painful path with me and for being my refuge. Amen.
