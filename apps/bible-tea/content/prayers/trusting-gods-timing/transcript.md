# Trusting God's Timing

Lord, I come to You with a heart full of questions, feeling the weight of decisions that seem to linger without resolution. I confess my impatience, my desire for answers now, and my struggle to wait in faith. You know how hard it is for me to trust when I can't see the path ahead. Yet, I remember Your promise that "for everything there is a season, and a time for every matter under heaven." I ask You to help me believe in Your perfect timing, even when it feels like my prayers are met with silence.

I lay my plans before You, acknowledging that Your ways are higher than mine. I ask for the courage to release my grip on what I think should happen and when. Give me peace in the waiting, and help me to see the beauty in the process, not just the destination. Guide my steps, Lord, and open my heart to Your wisdom. Let me not lean on my own understanding, but trust in Your unfailing love and guidance.

Strengthen my faith to rest in the knowledge that You are at work, even when I cannot see it. Teach me to embrace Your timing, knowing that You will make all things beautiful in their time. Thank You for being my constant, my guide, and my steadfast hope. Amen.
