# Prayer for Physical Healing

Father, I come before You feeling weak and broken. My body struggles under the weight of illness, and I long for Your healing touch. You have created me with such care, intricately woven together. I know that You see every part of me and understand my pain. Your word reminds me that You are the Lord who heals, and I hold onto that promise with all my heart.

When I am weary and burdened, You invite me to come to You for rest. I lay my worries and fears at Your feet, trusting that You are near to those who call on You in truth. Please, Lord, bring restoration to my body. Let Your healing power flow through every part of me, renewing and reviving what is broken.

You are my strength, my refuge in times of trouble. Even as I walk through this valley, I will not fear, for You are with me. Lord, grant me patience and courage as I wait for Your healing. Help me to find comfort in Your presence and to trust in Your perfect timing. Thank You for being my Healer and my hope. I place my body and my life in Your hands, knowing that You are able to do immeasurably more than I can ask or imagine. Amen.
