# Prayer for Provision

Dear Lord, I come before You, heart heavy with the weight of financial burdens. You know the bills that are due and the emptiness of my bank account. I feel so overwhelmed and anxious about how I will make ends meet. Father, I remember Your promise in Philippians 4:19, that You will supply all my needs according to Your riches in glory by Christ Jesus. Help me to trust that promise, even when my circumstances seem insurmountable.

I lay my fears and worries at Your feet, Lord, and ask for Your supernatural provision. You are the God who fed the multitudes with just a few loaves and fish, and I believe You can multiply my resources too. Please guide me in wisdom to manage what I have with grace and discernment. Show me opportunities I might not see, and open doors that seem firmly shut.

In this time of need, remind me that my worth is not tied to my wealth, and that You are my ultimate provider. Strengthen my faith, so I can remain steadfast, knowing You hold my future in Your hands. May I find peace in Your presence, even amid financial storms. Thank You for hearing my cries and for Your unfailing love. Amen.
