# A Thankful Heart

God, I come to You with a heart full of gratitude, even when life feels heavy. In these moments, I want to lift my eyes to You and see the blessings You shower upon me. I thank You for the breath in my lungs, for the sunrise that paints the sky, and for the small acts of kindness that whisper Your love to me. You remind me that "every good and perfect gift is from above," and for that, I am grateful.

Help me to see Your hand in the everyday and the ordinary. Even in seasons of difficulty, I choose to trust in Your goodness and faithfulness. I thank You for the lessons that come wrapped in challenges, for they teach me resilience and draw me closer to You. You are my refuge and my strength, and I praise You for being my constant source of hope.

God, cultivate in me a heart that overflows with thanksgiving. Let gratitude be my default, not only when life is smooth, but also when the road is rough. I want to live with a spirit of praise that honors You and acknowledges the beauty You weave into each day. Thank You for Your unending grace and love. Amen.
